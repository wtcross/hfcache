# AD-03 — Retain revisions observed by scheduled runs

- Status: accepted.
- ADR backfill: 2026-10-17; original decision and implementation dates are
  recorded below.
- Authority: the cited architecture contract remains normative. This ADR
  records rationale and history; maintain it under [the ADR policy](README.md).

## Context

Upstream references can advance several times between syncs. Capturing
every upstream commit would require a history reader or a substantially
different polling and resource model.

## Decision

Resolve the configured reference on each run. Publish a newly observed
SHA, retain the existing revision history according to policy, and do not
recover commits that appeared and were superseded between runs.

## Rationale and alternatives

The service's value is a recent complete revision and bounded observed
history. It does not promise a continuous record of all upstream commits.

The original rationale identifies aggressive polling and Git-history
traversal as ways to capture every commit; the adopted observation contract
does not require either.

## Consequences

Retention depth counts unique observed publications rather than elapsed
time or upstream commit count. Re-observing a retained SHA may move the
current reference without downloading it again or changing its retention
generation. Operators select observation frequency through the sync timer.
