# AD-13 — Repair an instance without redefining the revision

- Status: accepted.
- ADR backfill: 2026-10-17; original decision and implementation dates are
  recorded below.
- Authority: the cited architecture contract remains normative. This ADR
  records rationale and history; maintain it under [the ADR policy](README.md).

## Context

A damaged published tree may still have a valid original manifest and
consumers copying its immutable locator. Repair must recover expected
bytes without turning corruption into the new definition of correctness.

## Decision

Download the same SHA into a transaction-scoped isolated cache. Verify all
paths, sizes, and SHA-256 digests against the original manifest, publish a
new instance with a new publication ID, and reverify it at its published
location before switching references. Preserve the revision's generation
and retain the old instance at its locator for the seven-day grace period.

## Rationale and alternatives

The original manifest is evidence of what was archived. Replacing its
expected digests with newly observed values would make corruption appear
valid. A new locator and grace period protect in-flight consumer copies.

The decision expressly prohibits rewriting expected digests or replacing
an existing publication in place. It does not provide a separately ranked
list of repair implementations.

## Consequences

A mismatched or incomplete redownload fails with `HFRET006` and leaves the
published revision unchanged. Recovery can finish a partial two-reference
switch while both instances remain readable. Expired old instances become
quarantined evidence; storage-health confirmation and explicit removal
authority are required before discarding that evidence.
