# AD-05 — Order retention by immutable publication generation

- Status: accepted contract; known implementation gap.
- ADR backfill: 2026-10-17; original decision and implementation dates are
  recorded below.
- Authority: the cited architecture contract remains normative. This ADR
  records rationale and history; maintain it under [the ADR policy](../../../../../adr/README.md).

## Context

Clocks, filesystem mtimes, upstream timestamps, and restored snapshots can
contradict chronological assumptions. Retention must preserve its ordering
through repair, pruning, and crash recovery.

## Decision

Assign each new unique SHA a monotonic per-repository integer
`publication_generation` at first publication, under the writer lock. Order
retention only by that generation. Preserve it when repairing or
re-observing a SHA, and allocate against retained manifests plus
pruned-revision records.

## Rationale and alternatives

A generation assigned once is stable through recovery and independent of
clock errors. Including pruned audit evidence prevents a prune followed by
a crash from making an old generation available for reuse.

The decision explicitly excludes wall-clock time, file or directory
mtimes, and upstream commit timestamps as ordering signals. Those signals
can change after restores and do not establish publication identity.

## Consequences

Gaps are valid, while two distinct SHAs in a repository may never share a
generation. Repair does not make an old revision newest. Sorting and
selection remain deterministic even when timestamps disagree.

## Known implementation gap

As of this ADR backfill, `publication.next_generation()` reads only retained
references, rather than the required union with pruned audit records.
`sync._finish_ingest()` allocates a fresh generation when a previously
pruned SHA returns. The existing
`test_a_pruned_sha_that_returns_is_republished_into_a_fresh_container` even
expects that returned SHA to receive generation 7. These behaviors do not
satisfy AD-05/FS-20's original-generation and audit-aware allocation contract.
The contract remains unchanged; pending `RET-011` in
[the progress tracker](../../../../progress.md) tracks explicit contract/code
reconciliation. Resolving the intended rule and allocator requires a new
recorded decision and aligned implementation and regression coverage.
