Adapter source boundaries reviewed:
  - Fixed privileged argv is exactly
    /usr/local/libexec/hfcache-container-service inspect-cockpit; no inputs,
    executable/path/manager selection or container start is accepted.
  - Inspection streams at most 16 KiB of retained data; helper/process failure
    is not parsed as successful inspection. UI facts never authorize effects.
  - requireHostAction performs a fresh inspection, checks trusted host/session,
    and refuses an older response superseded by newer profile facts.
  - Every mutating D-Bus call, including Reload, requires fresh permission.
    Observer stop and timer scheduling use separate permission categories.
    EnableUnitFiles uses runtime=false and force=false; no forced replacement
    or unmask path is added. Its exact D-Bus assertion belongs to the separately
    recorded mounted adapter checks.
  - Drop-in write/removal require fresh schedule proof before file effects.
    Writes validate current calendar and optional delay with fixed read-only
    systemd-analyze argv, then refresh permission immediately before writing.
    Positional expressions follow --. Session/host changes refuse before effect;
    no mutation is replayed.
  - Schedule UI gates all controls, prevents duplicate confirmation submission
    synchronously and discards older calendar-check results after input/session
    changes. Delay edits invalidate the earlier calendar check. Schedule reads
    are scoped to both the captured host and connection generation.
  - Pending and uncertain attempts survive tab unmount in application memory:
    at most eight host records, each at most 8 KiB of expected drop-in bytes,
    with a conservative aggregate 64 KiB budget including retained safe error
    allowance. No unresolved record is evicted to make room for another host.
  - After an acknowledged write/removal, later Reload/read/restart refusal
    requires explicit read-only inspection of the exact captured bytes/null
    and a known unmasked fixed timer, then separate acknowledgement. Inspection
    and acknowledgement perform no Reload/restart and do not prove the loaded
    calendar or success of the prior manager action. Original safe error and
    policy refusal remain visible; another calendar check cannot clear the latch.
  - Hook refresh after a generation change during an in-flight probe does not
    get stuck on the old request. A failed new Doctor run cannot retain an old
    successful card result as its current result.
